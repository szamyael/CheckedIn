in your pocket.

STUDENTS · START WITH YOUR CAMPUS ACCOUNT
04

Event check-in

A clear path
from QR to done.

Check-in is tied to the event and its attendance window. Keep the app open and follow each prompt.

1

Open Scan. Choose Scan in the app or student portal and allow camera access when asked.

2

Scan the event QR. Use the code displayed by the organizer. Check that you selected the right event.

3

Complete required checks. Depending on the event, confirm your location, enter the organizer's current OTP, or take a selfie.

4

Wait for confirmation. Look for a successful check-in message; review your history later in Profile.

Offline on mobile?

If offline attendance is available, a submission is saved on your device and waits to sync when connectivity returns. It may need staff review before it is confirmed.

If a scan fails, check the event's time window and follow the on-screen instructions. Ask the organizer for help—never reuse an expired OTP.

05

Staff quick start

Set up. Monitor.
Learn.

The staff workspace brings event operations and campus participation into one place.

1

Sign in to Staff. Use the account and role granted by your administrator.

2

Publish an event. Organization teams add its details, venue, schedule, attendance window, and required checks.

3

Share the check-in. Display the event QR and, when configured, the current OTP for attendees.

4

Monitor and review. Use Live Monitor for arriving check-ins; review reports and engagement after the event.

Organization teams

Create and manage their events and participation activities.

Faculty

Use the campus tools enabled for their assigned role.

Administrators

Oversee campus users, access, and platform operations.

Before doors open: confirm the event is published, the attendance window is correct, and the QR display is visible to attendees.
